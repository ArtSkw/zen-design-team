Music

Four ways the room could have music. None is a track: each is played as it goes, note by note, by a few rules, in the room's own key (the yo scale on D, the key of the arrival's kalimba and the bowl), so it never comes round the same way twice. Like the Vibes app, two dials — how much is played, and how near it sounds. Put the world under it to hear it as the room would. Every note blooms below as a ring on the water. The breath and the lake can also be played by ElevenLabs recordings (Instrument): the same take, by another hand.
